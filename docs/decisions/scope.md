# Study scope and governance decision

Decision date: 2026-10-01

Decision owner: Mohammad Zoraiz

Status: accepted for internal computational work; the explicitly pending reviews and access items below are not approvals.

## Main scientific scope

The main study is simulation-first and quasi-static. Loading increments, minimizations, and rupture events are history-dependent load/event coordinates, not elapsed biological time. The primary research target is closed-loop prediction of deformation, mechanical redistribution, and irreversible physical-topology change, with demonstrated generalization, physical consistency, uncertainty quality, and measured efficiency against fair mechanistic and learned baselines.

Physical-time/rate claims are outside the primary scope. Relaxation times, fatigue lifetimes in seconds, biological time-to-failure, and rate dependence require a separately activated and validated dynamics extension. A learned model is not described as more accurate than the reference simulator on labels generated by that simulator.

- Treat the two-edge refinement interpretation as a provisional axial-mechanics hypothesis, not confirmed molecular coarse-graining. With inherited `K=5570`, one LAMMPS edge has force tangent `11140 pN/nm`; two identical collinear edges in series have force tangent `5570 pN/nm` and combined rest length `2.06 nm`.

- The fixed-cell minimized state is the primary `fixed_cell_equilibrated` reference. It is not zero tension. Preserve its coordinates, full cell matrix, achieved geometry, minimization diagnostics, and initial configurational tension.
- Report total configurational 2D membrane tension as primary and incremental tension from the fixed reference as secondary. Use `pN/nm` internally and `N/m` in summaries. No 3D stress/modulus conversion is allowed without an approved thickness and uncertainty.
- Quasi-static pressure/tension uses the configurational virial, excluding kinetic pressure. The legacy mass remains a serialization placeholder and does not validate physical time.
- Computational axes are `x=axial`, `y=hoop`. Laboratory mapping remains dataset-specific and unconfirmed. Cylindrical display wraps `y` and retains `x` along the axis, labeled as 2D mechanics displayed on a cylindrical surface.

## Failure and rupture scope

The primary scalar endpoint is `damage_initiation`: the first mechanically triggered, irreversible physical-bond rupture after a valid reference preparation under a versioned phenomenological rupture law. User-prescribed removal, computational neighbor changes, rendering changes, initial invalid states, and solver errors do not count. No event within the tested load range is right-censored.

Continue trajectories after first rupture and report cascades, redistribution, load/stiffness degradation, connectivity change, and instability as distinct outcomes. Connectivity is not rigidity, and minimizer failure is not physical failure. Initial laws may use deterministic thresholds or quenched heterogeneous thresholds sampled once per physical bond from the material-disorder stream; no fresh lottery is drawn at each minimization or retry. Threshold values remain provisional and versioned.

## Experimental target and access boundary

Training remains simulation-first. The proposed experimental target is a small, lab-approved quasi-static AFM force-indentation cohort for an untreated laboratory *E. coli* baseline with matched morphology, plus complementary purified-sacculus structural imaging where appropriate. Exact strain, preparation, live/fixed/isolated state, medium/buffer, temperature, osmotic conditions, substrate, tip/calibration/acquisition settings, and specimen/session IDs remain unknown and must not be invented.

The authorized custodian, approved storage location, permitted uses, protocol, training, acquisition owner, and release restrictions are not confirmed. These remain blocked under P08-01 and must be requested through Prof. Schmidt and the relevant data creator. Private experimental files and derivatives remain outside Git, external services, and public artifacts until actual permission is recorded.

Whole-cell AFM comparison requires an explicit observation chain from the PG network to anisotropic membrane response to a validated curved-shell/contact calculation. Geometry, prestress/turgor, bending, tip/substrate contact, and non-PG envelope contributions must be included or bounded. Isotropic box strain is not relabeled as turgor loading.

## Compute and release boundaries

Internal implementation, tests, and bounded pilots are authorized in this workspace. Initial ceilings are one heavy simulation/training process, at most two compute threads, at most `min(4 GiB, 25% installed RAM)`, 10 minutes per smoke command, 30 minutes per explicitly identified pilot, two cumulative heavy-compute hours before a new estimate, 5 GiB new results while retaining at least 20% free disk, and one diagnosed transient retry. Reduce these ceilings if the machine cannot support them.

Cluster production, paid cloud resources, public deployment, external private-data processing, public artifact release, and manuscript submission are not authorized. Production limits require measured pilot runtime, peak memory, storage per trajectory, convergence/failure rate, and an explicit later approval. Mohammad approves any final release/submission; lab-derived materials also require their actual rights holders. Git commit identity does not determine manuscript authorship.

## Primary-source checks

The computational facts above were checked against [Nguyen et al. (PNAS 2015)](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1504281112) and official LAMMPS documentation for [harmonic bonds](https://docs.lammps.org/bond_harmonic.html), [nonlinear bonds](https://docs.lammps.org/bond_nonlinear.html), [2D pressure](https://docs.lammps.org/compute_pressure.html), and [box relaxation](https://docs.lammps.org/fix_box_relax.html). Experimental interpretation remains cautious because [whole-cell envelope mechanics include substantial outer-membrane contributions](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30022160/) and structural imaging does not supply bond-level rupture labels.
